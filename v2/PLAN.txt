TEACHING CONTRACT
Deep dive = contract and prerequisites; one continuous numerical trace;
state/invariant; correctness argument; time and auxiliary space; failing
alternative; complete runnable program; transfer exercise and separate answer.
Introduction = mechanism and one trace, explicitly not implementation mastery.
Roadmap = when useful, prerequisites and follow-up practice; no mastery claim.
Division names describe approximate learning groups, not an official syllabus.

ENTERTAINMENT / VISUAL DESIGN
Rohail, a fictional tenth grader, helps Maya and Sam organize a school music
night. Use believable ticket refunds, rehearsal choices, group constraints and
equipment budgets. Large contest bounds are hypothetical stress tests, not
claims about actual school attendance. No cow or mascot in active V2.
Every joke must reveal a misconception or motivate a state change. Queue
animation is an entry line; priority is distance, not follower count.
Backtracking is a set-list audition with visible undo. Warm original synthetic
narration requires a listening audition; the earlier flat voice was rejected.
Original rap transitions recap an invariant in plain language immediately after.
Song titles and factual high-level Drake–Kendrick rivalry references can frame
competition. Selected clean user-supplied excerpts may be adapted as short
pedagogical hooks with original explanations; no additional lyric retrieval,
recordings, melody copying, artist voice imitation or implied endorsement.
