V2 WORKING EXERCISES — solutions are in SOLUTIONS.txt
These are original course exercises unless an official link is given.

1. Execution and invariant
Trace [-4,0,7,-3]. Record i and total before every condition check, including
the final false check. Explain why n=0 prints zero. Write a one-element input
that distinguishes summing all values from summing only positive values.
Then change the task to count negative values. State the new invariant first.

2. Overflow
Two int variables both hold 1,500,000,000. Explain why assigning their sum to
long long does not necessarily make the expression safe. Supply a safe expression.
For n<=100000 and |a[i]|<=10^9, prove a long long running total is sufficient.

3. Backtracking
Count subsets of indices of [5,-3] summing to 2. Draw both branches at each
position, label (i,sum), and mark the leaves. Explain why pruning whenever
sum exceeds target loses a valid answer. Are the two singleton subsets of
[2,2] different when counting subsets of indices? What about the empty subset?
Extension: maintain a chosen-index vector with push/recurse/pop; explain why
passing sum by value does not require an explicit subtraction on return.

4. Parity constraints
Four vertices: 1 same as 2; 2 different from 3; vertex 4 isolated.
Find the number of assignments and write it in binary. Add edge 1 same as 3:
what changes? Explain why one arbitrary root color per component is sufficient
to detect inconsistency, and why flipping every color preserves the constraints.
Give a self-loop that is impossible and one that is harmless.
